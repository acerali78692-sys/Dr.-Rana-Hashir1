How treatment
actually works

Every plan follows the same four stages — in this order, every time.

  1. 01

    Assess

    A full movement and history review to find the actual source of the pain — often not where it hurts.

  2. 02

    Treat

    Hands-on manual therapy, dry needling, or targeted mobilisation to reduce pain and restore range of motion.

  3. 03

    Move

    A progressive exercise program, adjusted week to week as strength and confidence return.

  4. 04

    Maintain

    A short home routine designed to keep you out of the clinic — that's the actual goal.

About Dr. Hashir

Rana Hashir is a Doctor of Physical Therapy with over four years of clinical experience treating musculoskeletal and sports injuries. After years of watching patients get generic advice online, he started sharing short, practical rehab tips — the same exercises he gives in the clinic, minus the wait.

His approach favours movement over rest wherever it's safe to do so, and he works closely with each patient's own goals, whether that's returning to sport, sitting through a workday pain-free, or simply picking up a grandchild again.
